n8n Pricing 2026 — Cloud vs Self-Hosted Cost

n8n is the source-available automation tool developers reach for. You can pay for managed Cloud, run it on a cheap VPS, or host it on your own hardware for the price of the electricity. Here's the real cost of each path.

Prices verified June 2026 · taken from n8n's official pricing · all figures per run

As of June 2026, the cheapest plan we track for n8n costs $8 per month at 1,000 runs and $45 at 100,000 runs (cheapest qualifying plan, 3-step workflows, monthly billing), from n8n's official pricing, verified by hand. Self-hosting is free — the figure is the server it runs on, not a tool fee.

Heads up: n8n's self-hosted edition is free, source-available software (Sustainable Use License) — the figures below for VPS and own-server are infrastructure cost (server, database, electricity), not a licence fee. They assume light workflows and exclude your own time to run it.

Frequently asked questions

On the cheapest qualifying plan (3-step workflows, monthly billing) n8n runs $8 at 1,000 runs, $8 at 5,000 runs, $25 at 20,000 runs and $45 at 100,000 runs. See the plans and comparison table above for the full breakdown.
At 20,000 runs/month the cheapest pick we track is Activepieces at $0 vs n8n at $25. Use the calculator for your exact volume.
The software is free under n8n's Sustainable Use licence. You still pay for somewhere to run it — a small VPS, or your own hardware plus electricity. Both are far below cloud-tool pricing, which is why self-hosting wins on cost at scale.
VPS means renting a small cloud server billed monthly. Own-server means running n8n on hardware you already own (a mini PC or Raspberry Pi) — the only recurring cost is electricity, with a one-off hardware outlay shown separately below.
