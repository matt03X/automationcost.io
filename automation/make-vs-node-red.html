Make vs Node-RED — pricing & features compared

Make vs Node-RED
Two visual tools with different roots. Node-RED is open-source (Apache 2.0), self-hosted and built for event-driven and IoT/hardware flows, so its cost is just your server; Make is a managed cloud platform priced per operation with 3,000+ SaaS integrations and a drag-and-drop canvas. Node-RED fits developers wiring devices and APIs; Make fits teams automating SaaS apps without hosting anything. See the price table below for figures.
