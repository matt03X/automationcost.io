Automation tool pricing — what actually changed, 2024-06 – 2026-06

Across the major automation platforms, core pricing has barely moved in two years. Here is the evidence — every claim links to a dated Web Archive snapshot.

The short version
  • Zapier — core plans ($0 / $19.99 / $69) unchanged for 2+ years.
  • Make — annual prices ($9 / $16 / $29) flat since at least Feb 2025.
  • n8n — one real overhaul (Aug 2025): workflow limits removed, Business tier added.
  • Pipedream — Basic / Advanced ($29 / $49) flat; free tier cut 300 → 100 credits.

n8n

One real overhaul (Aug 2025): workflow limits removed + Business tier
35 archived snapshots · 2024-06 → 2026-06
Starter$20 / mobilled annually, 2.5k executions / mo · stable since Aug 2025 (EUR today)
Pro$50 / mobilled annually, 10k executions / mo · stable since Aug 2025 (EUR today)
Business$667 / mobilled annually · stable since Aug 2025 (EUR today)
2025-08-07Real change
Major overhaul — workflow limits removed, Business tier added

Until 6 Aug 2025: Starter $24/$20 (monthly/annual) capped at 5 active workflows; Pro $60/$50 capped at 15; plus a $120/mo tier (50k exec / 50 workflows). On 7 Aug 2025 the workflow/step/user limits were removed (now unlimited), quotas were rewritten to annual, the $120 tier was discontinued and a Business plan ($667 / $800 annual / monthly) appeared.

2025-09Display only
Monthly variants dropped from the page (display change, not prices)

The page stopped rendering the monthly figures (24 / 60 / 800) in HTML and defaulted to annual only. The annual prices themselves did not change.

⚠ Coverage gap 2024-06 → 2025-03: Pricing rendered client-side in this window — archived HTML has no figures.
❓ Open question — Currency: the Aug 2025 archive showed USD ($24 / $20); the live page today is in EUR (€24 / €20). When (or whether) the $→€ switch happened can't be pinned from the archive.
