Zapier vs n8n — which costs less?

n8n < Zapier at every volume we track — self-hosted
n8n self-hosted is the cheaper option at every volume we track — but only if you self-host it; Zapier is the no-code path with 9,000+ integrations. n8n's Community Edition software is free; you pay only for the server, which scales from ~$8/mo on a small VPS up to ~$66/mo for high volume — hardware, not a tool fee. If nobody on your team wants to own a server, compare against n8n Cloud ($24–60/mo at low volume) instead: still cheaper than Zapier's $73–$733 across the same range, but no longer a rout.
