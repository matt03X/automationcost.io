As of June 2026, the cheapest plan we track for Zapier costs $73.5 per month at 1,000 runs and $1,149 at 100,000 runs (cheapest qualifying plan, 3-step workflows, monthly billing), from Zapier's official pricing, verified by hand.

Zapier charges per task (each action-step execution), not per Zap. A 3-step Zap that runs 100 times costs 300 tasks (the trigger doesn't count). On this site we normalise that to runs × steps so you can compare it like-for-like with other tools — which is why a multi-step Zap gets expensive fast.
