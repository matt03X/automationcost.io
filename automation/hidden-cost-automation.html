The hidden costs of automation tools

The price shown on an automation tool's pricing page is rarely the price you end up paying. Volume limits, overage tiers and annual billing premiums can add 20–50% on top of the headline number — before you've even started. This page maps out the three hidden costs that most often catch teams off guard, using the same data that powers our calculator.

Self-hosted tools (n8n, Activepieces, Automatisch, Node-RED) have no ops ceiling at all: the limit is your server capacity.

3. The self-hosted escape hatch

If overage charges and billing lock-in are a concern, the cleanest exit is switching to a self-hostable tool. n8n, Activepieces, Automatisch, Node-RED are free to run — the only recurring cost is the server it runs on. (Licensing differs: Automatisch and Node-RED are open-source; Activepieces is open-core; n8n is fair-code under its Sustainable Use License — all are free to self-host.)

Our self-hosted automation cost guide models VPS costs from entry level up to high-volume workloads and compares them directly against paid cloud plans. The short version: at most volumes self-hosting is cheaper than paid cloud, and there are no overages by design.
